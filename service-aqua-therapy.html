Aqua Therapy / Hydrotherapy Swimming Pool

Our specialized aqua therapy and hydrotherapy pools are designed for rehabilitation, wellness, and therapeutic treatments. These pools incorporate specific features like adjustable water temperature, resistance jets, and ergonomic designs to aid in physical therapy.

Key Features

  • Temperature-controlled water system
  • Resistance jets for exercise therapy
  • Ergonomic seating and handrails
  • Non-slip surfaces for safety
  • Wheelchair accessible options
  • Adjustable depth configurations
  • UV water purification system
  • Suitable for hospitals & wellness centers

Hydrotherapy has been proven to help with muscle recovery, joint pain relief, cardiovascular health, and mental wellness. Our therapy pools are designed in consultation with medical professionals to ensure they meet the highest standards for therapeutic use.
