Swimming Pool Pipeless Filter

We have developed advanced pipeless filters incorporating the latest technology. As a leading manufacturer, we offer a wide range of pipeless filters in various sizes and designs. Pipeless filters use pleated polyester cartridge that effectively traps debris. They operate with lower water flow and pressure, reducing energy consumption. Minimal maintenance eliminates frequent backwashing, lowering long-term costs.
