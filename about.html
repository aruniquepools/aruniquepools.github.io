AR Unique Pools

India's Leading Fiberglass Composite Swimming Pool Manufacturer

AR UNIQUE POOLS is a leading manufacturer of fiberglass composite swimming pools in India, offering over 10 exclusive models and designs. We are proud to have introduced designer swimming pools with glass mosaic and stone finishes to the industry — a true blend of beauty, technology, and innovation.

Our mission is to provide world-class swimming pool solutions that combine aesthetics, durability, and functionality. With years of experience and a dedicated team of experts, we ensure every project is completed with perfection.
